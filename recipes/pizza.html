Homemade Pizza

Description

Crispy crust, cheesy topping - better than delivery.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 3/4 cup warm water
  • 1 tsp yeast, 1 tsp sugar, 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • Mozzarella, pepperoni, bell peppers

Steps

  1. Mix warm water, sugar, yeast - wait 5 mins.
  2. Add flour, salt, oil - knead 8 mins, rest 1 hour.
  3. Roll dough into circle.
  4. Spread sauce, add cheese and toppings.
  5. Bake at 220°C for 12-15 mins.
