Jollof Rice

Description

West Africa's most famous party rice - smoky, spicy, and full of flavor.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long grain rice
  • 3 large tomatoes, 2 red bell peppers, 2 onions, 2 scotch bonnets
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp thyme, curry, 2 bay leaves, seasoning cubes
  • 2 cups chicken stock

Steps

  1. Blend tomatoes, peppers, 1 onion, and scotch bonnet until smooth.
  2. Heat oil, fry onions and tomato paste for 3 mins.
  3. Pour blended mix, add spices, fry for 15 mins till thick.
  4. Add stock, bring to boil, add washed rice.
  5. Cover with foil and lid, cook on low heat for 30 mins.
  6. Stir in sliced onions and serve hot.
