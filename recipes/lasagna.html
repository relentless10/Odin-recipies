Lasagna

Description

Cheesy, meaty layers of pasta baked to perfection.

Ingredients

  • 9 lasagna sheets
  • 500g minced beef
  • 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves
  • 400g tomato sauce
  • 2 cups bechamel sauce
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Fry onion and garlic, add beef till brown.
  2. Add tomato sauce and simmer 15 mins.
  3. Boil lasagna sheets for 8 mins.
  4. Layer sauce, sheets, white sauce, cheese - repeat 3 times.
  5. Top with extra cheese and bake at 180°C for 30 mins.
