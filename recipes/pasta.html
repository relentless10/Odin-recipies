Creamy Pasta

Description

Quick 15-minute creamy pasta for busy days.

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 cup cream
  • 1/2 cup parmesan
  • Salt, pepper, parsley

Steps

  1. Boil pasta in salted water, drain.
  2. Melt butter, fry garlic 30 seconds.
  3. Add cream and parmesan, stir till thick.
  4. Add pasta and mix well.
  5. Add pepper and parsley and serve.
